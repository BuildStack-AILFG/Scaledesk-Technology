The Instagram app was designed for one person and one account. The moment two or three colleagues need to answer customers, problems appear: two people reply to the same message, another message is missed entirely, and nobody knows what was already promised. A shared inbox solves this.

## The problems a shared inbox fixes

- **Duplicate replies.** Two people answer the same customer differently.
- **Missed messages.** A message arrives when nobody is looking, and it is forgotten.
- **No history.** A colleague takes over and has no idea what was discussed.
- **Poor visibility.** A manager cannot see how fast the team responds.
- **Lost leads.** Someone shared a phone number in a DM and it never reached the CRM.

## What a good shared inbox includes

1. **One place for messages and comments.** Direct messages, story replies and comments together.
2. **Assignment.** Every conversation has an owner, and unassigned ones are obvious.
3. **Status.** Open, waiting on customer, resolved.
4. **Internal notes.** Colleagues can leave context that the customer never sees.
5. **Saved replies.** Answers to common questions, ready to insert and edit.
6. **History.** The full conversation is visible to whoever picks it up.
7. **Reporting.** Response times, volume and outcomes.

## Set simple team rules

Tools help, but rules make the difference.

- **Response target.** For example, first reply within ten minutes during working hours.
- **Ownership.** Whoever replies first owns the conversation until it is handed off.
- **Hand-off etiquette.** Leave a note explaining what was discussed and what the customer expects next.
- **Tone guide.** A short document with example replies keeps the voice consistent.
- **Escalation.** Decide who deals with complaints, refunds and unusual requests.

## Use automation for the first response

An automated greeting covers nights and busy periods. It should tell the customer when a person will reply and, where possible, answer the most common questions. Then your team steps in with context. ## Measure what matters

- **First response time.**
- **Resolution time.**
- **Conversations per agent**, to balance workload.
- **Leads created** from Instagram and their outcomes.
- **Customer satisfaction**, if you collect it.

## A short checklist to start

1. List everyone who answers messages today.
2. Choose a shared inbox and connect your professional account.
3. Write five saved replies for your most common questions.
4. Agree a response target and who owns escalations.
5. Test for a week, then review the numbers together.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can several people use one Instagram account?

Yes, when the account is connected to a platform that supports multiple users, each person works in the shared inbox instead of logging into the Instagram app.

### Do customers see who replied?

Customers see messages from your business account. Internal notes and assignments stay private to your team.

### How many people should share an inbox?

As many as you need, provided ownership is clear. The rule is that every conversation has exactly one owner at a time.
