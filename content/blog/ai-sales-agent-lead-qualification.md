Most businesses lose leads not because their product is poor, but because they respond too slowly or stop following up too soon. An AI sales agent is designed to fix that gap: replying quickly, asking the right questions and keeping conversations moving until a person can take over.

## The problem it addresses

- A lead arrives at 9 p.m. and gets no reply until the next afternoon.
- A salesperson meant to follow up gets busy and forgets.
- Leads with poor fit consume time that should go to the best ones.
- Nobody records what was said, so the next person starts from scratch.

## What an AI sales agent can do

1. **Reply within minutes**, at any hour, on the channel the lead used.
2. **Ask qualifying questions** about need, budget, timing and location.
3. **Answer common questions** about products, prices and process.
4. **Book meetings or calls** in your team's calendar.
5. **Follow up** if the lead goes quiet, with a limited number of helpful messages.
6. **Record everything** in your CRM, including a summary.
7. **Hand over warm leads** to a person, with context.

## What it should leave to people

- Negotiating price and terms.
- Handling objections that need judgement.
- Building relationships on high-value deals.
- Anything the customer asks a person to handle.

## Design the qualification questions

Keep them short and conversational. Two or three are enough at first:

- "What are you looking for?"
- "When would you like to start?"
- "Roughly what budget range are you considering?"

Use the answers to score the lead. For a simple approach, read [lead scoring basics](/blog/lead-scoring-basics-for-small-teams).

## Design the hand-off

Decide what makes a lead ready for a person, for example a confirmed need plus a budget in range, or a request for a quote or call. When that happens:

- Notify the owner immediately.
- Give a short summary and the full conversation.
- Tell the customer who will be in touch and when.

See [hand-off design](/blog/whatsapp-chatbot-with-human-handoff) for detailed advice.

## Follow up respectfully

A helpful sequence might be: a reply within minutes, a follow-up the next day, and a lighter reminder after a few days, then stop. Vary the messages and always offer a way to opt out. We explain the pattern in [a 7-day follow-up sequence](/blog/whatsapp-lead-follow-up-sequence).

## Keep it on brand and honest

- Write instructions in your brand's voice.
- Do not let it promise prices, dates or discounts it cannot verify.
- Say when a person will take over.
- Review conversations weekly and correct problems.

Our guide to [AI agent guardrails](/blog/ai-agent-guardrails-human-in-the-loop) provides a checklist.

## Measure the results

- Time to first response.
- Percentage of leads contacted.
- Meetings booked.
- Conversion by source.
- Quality of hand-offs, judged by the salespeople.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will leads know they are talking to an AI?

They should be told. Clear disclosure with an easy path to a person builds trust.

### Does an AI sales agent replace salespeople?

No. It handles the fast first response and routine questions, so your salespeople can spend time on the conversations that need them.

### How many follow-up messages are too many?

Two or three well-spaced messages are usually enough. Stop when the lead asks you to.
