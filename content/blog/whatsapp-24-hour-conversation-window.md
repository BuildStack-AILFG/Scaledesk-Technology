If you use the WhatsApp Business Platform, the single most important rule to understand is the 24-hour customer service window. It explains why some messages send freely and others are blocked or need a template, and it shapes how you design every automated follow-up.

## The rule in one sentence

When a customer sends you a message, a 24-hour window opens. During that window you can reply with normal, free-form messages. When the window closes, you can only send a pre-approved **template message** to start the conversation again.

## What resets the window

Each new message from the customer restarts the 24 hours. If a customer writes to you at 9 am, you can reply freely until 9 am the next day. If they write again at 6 pm, the window now runs until 6 pm the next day.

Your own messages do not extend the window. Only the customer's messages do.

## What you can send inside the window

- Normal text replies, including long explanations.
- Images, documents, voice notes and other media.
- Interactive messages such as buttons and lists.
- Answers from a human agent or an automated flow, without any template.

## What you can send outside the window

Only approved templates. These are messages you write in advance and submit for review, such as an appointment reminder or an order update. See our [template guide](/blog/whatsapp-message-templates-guide) for categories and tips. The customer must also have opted in to hear from you, which we cover in [WhatsApp opt-in best practices](/blog/whatsapp-opt-in-best-practices).

## Why it matters for automation

The window shapes three common designs:

1. **Follow-ups.** If a lead goes quiet for more than a day, your follow-up must be a template. Plan those templates in advance.
2. **Slow replies.** If your team answers after 24 hours, you can no longer send a free-form reply, only a template. Fast first responses matter.
3. **Campaigns.** Broadcasts to many customers are always template messages, because you are starting the conversation.

## Practical tips

- **Answer within the window whenever you can.** Free-form replies are more flexible and feel more personal.
- **Use templates that invite a reply.** A template that ends with a question or a button brings the customer back and opens a fresh window.
- **Show your team the countdown.** Many inboxes display how long is left in each chat, so agents know which conversations are urgent.
- **Do not chase repeatedly.** Two or three well-timed templates beat ten pushy ones, and too many unwelcome messages hurt your quality rating.
- **Log everything in your CRM.** When a chat crosses the 24-hour mark, your CRM should still show its history and next step. A tool such as [LeadForGrow](/products/leadforgrow-crm) keeps that record alongside the conversation.

## A simple example

A customer messages on Monday at 10 am asking about a service. Your automated flow answers at once and asks for their preferred date. They reply at 11 am. The window is now open until 11 am Tuesday. If they do not reply again, your Tuesday afternoon follow-up must be a template, such as "Hi, are you still interested in booking? Tap below to choose a slot."

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the 24-hour window apply to the free WhatsApp Business app?

The window rule applies to the WhatsApp Business Platform. On the free app you can message people freely, but you also cannot automate at scale.

### Can I send a message after 24 hours without a template?

No. After the window closes, you need an approved template to start the conversation again, and the customer must have opted in.

### Does an automated reply count as my message?

Yes, but your messages do not extend the window. Only the customer's messages restart the 24 hours.
