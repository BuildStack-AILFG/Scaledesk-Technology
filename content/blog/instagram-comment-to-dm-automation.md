You have probably seen posts that say "Comment GUIDE and we'll send it to you". Behind those posts is comment-to-DM automation: when someone comments a keyword, a message is sent to their inbox. Done thoughtfully, it is a helpful way to share information. Done carelessly, it feels like spam and can put your account at risk.

## How it works

1. You publish a post that invites a specific comment, such as "Comment PRICE for details".
2. Software watches the comments on that post.
3. When a comment matches, it sends the person a private message, often a reply to their comment.
4. The conversation continues in the inbox, where your automation or your team can respond.

The rules of the platform decide how and when you can send that first private message. Meta has allowed businesses to send a private reply to a comment for a limited time after it is posted, and generally one per comment. Confirm the current rules in Meta's official documentation before building anything.

## When it is a good idea

- **Sharing a resource** people asked for, such as a price list, a brochure or a checklist.
- **Answering popular questions** without cluttering the comments.
- **Starting a sales conversation** with someone who has shown interest.
- **Handling launches or giveaways** where hundreds of people comment.

## When to avoid it

- **Asking for comments just to game the algorithm** with no real value behind them.
- **Sending unrelated promotions** to people who only wanted an answer.
- **Using it on posts where comments are sensitive**, such as complaints.

## Good practice

1. **Be clear in the post.** Tell people exactly what happens: "Comment GUIDE and we will DM you the link."
2. **Send what you promised.** The first message should deliver the value immediately, then ask a helpful follow-up question.
3. **Reply publicly too.** A short public reply such as "Sent, check your DMs" shows people it worked.
4. **Keep the message short and personal.** Use their name if you can.
5. **Give an easy way out.** People should be able to stop replies with a single word.
6. **Do not repeat.** One private reply per comment is the limit, and more will annoy people.
7. **Hand over to a person** when the conversation moves beyond the script.

## Example

**Post:** "Free checklist: 10 things to check before choosing a CRM. Comment CHECKLIST and we will send it."

**Private reply:** "Hi Anita, here is the checklist you asked for: [link]. Are you comparing tools right now or planning ahead? I can share a few pointers if you tell me your team size."

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I send a DM to anyone who comments?

The rules allow a private reply to a comment within a limited time, generally one per comment. Check Meta's current documentation for details.

### Will comment automation hurt my reach?

Asking for comments purely to boost engagement can feel spammy. Offer genuine value and people respond well.

### Do people need to follow me first?

Requirements can change and depend on the account and features. Check the current platform documentation.
